HOW TO CHOOSE

Choose for the task.

01

For meeting notes, check recording consent, transcription accuracy and export options.

02

For automation, test failure handling, permissions and what happens when a connected app changes.

03

For knowledge work, compare collaboration features, data controls and whether the tool fits your existing stack.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Should I automate a task immediately?

First document the steps and exceptions, then test automation on low-risk examples before relying on it.

What matters most for meeting assistants?

Consent, accuracy, speaker attribution, integrations and clear controls over recordings and transcripts.
