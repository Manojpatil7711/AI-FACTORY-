PRACTICAL EVALUATION GUIDE

How to use Gemini with care.

Who it may suit: People who use Google services; learners exploring explanations; creators planning content from text and supported media inputs. This guide describes possible workflows, not a promise that every feature is available to every account. Gemini is a product from Google; check the provider's own documentation for current capabilities, supported regions, plan limits and terms. The right choice depends on the task, your experience, the sensitivity of the material and how much review you can provide. Begin with a small, low-risk test rather than moving an important workflow immediately.

Three concrete use cases

1) Brainstorm a video outline from a topic and target audience. 2) Compare a set of ideas and ask for a table of trade-offs. 3) Use supported input types to organise notes, then verify conclusions against original materials.

Potential advantages

Convenient general assistant; supports a range of tasks and input formats depending on current version; can help organise early-stage work. For a fair test, use the same brief, source material and success criteria when comparing tools. Record the time needed to reach an acceptable result, not just the first response. A tool that produces a fast draft may still require substantial editing, fact-checking, formatting or human approval.

Limitations and trade-offs

Answers can be inaccurate or incomplete; integrations and features may differ by account, region and plan; review privacy settings before sharing material. Do not treat generated text, images, summaries, recommendations or code as automatically correct. Check names, figures, citations, calculations, accessibility, spelling and any claim that could affect a person or business. Keep a human reviewer responsible for final decisions.

Alternatives to compare

Two alternatives worth evaluating are ChatGPT and Claude. They may suit different budgets, workflows or preferences; this directory does not rank one as universally best. Compare the current free access, paid limits, export options, collaboration, supported languages, cancellation terms and data controls directly on each official website. Do not assume that a free tier includes commercial rights or the same features as a paid plan.

Who should not use it as their only solution

Avoid relying on Gemini alone when a task requires guaranteed factual accuracy, regulated professional judgement, confidential information without approved safeguards, or a legally binding decision. It may also be a poor fit if you need predictable outputs without review, offline operation, a specific integration that has not been confirmed, or a licence that the provider does not clearly grant. In those situations, use a qualified professional, an approved enterprise workflow or a conventional tool with controls that meet the requirement.

Before you sign up

Read the provider's privacy and terms pages before uploading customer records, private documents, unpublished work or another person's voice or image. Check retention and training settings, access permissions, deletion options and whether your intended use is permitted. If you are evaluating a paid plan, verify the live price, billing currency, taxes, usage caps and renewal conditions at checkout; AI FACTORY does not guarantee or publish fixed prices here. Test the output with a real but non-sensitive example, then decide whether the time saved is worth the cost.
