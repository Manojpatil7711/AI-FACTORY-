A PRACTICAL START

How to get started with Perplexity.

  1. Ask a focused question and include the scope or date range that matters.
  2. Open cited sources rather than relying on the generated summary alone.
  3. Compare primary or authoritative sources, especially when claims conflict or are time-sensitive.
CHECK BEFORE YOU COMMIT

PRACTICAL EVALUATION GUIDE

How to use Perplexity with care.

Who it may suit: Researchers beginning an unfamiliar topic; professionals tracking public information; students learning how to locate and inspect references. This guide describes possible workflows, not a promise that every feature is available to every account. Perplexity is a product from Perplexity AI; check the provider's own documentation for current capabilities, supported regions, plan limits and terms. The right choice depends on the task, your experience, the sensitivity of the material and how much review you can provide. Begin with a small, low-risk test rather than moving an important workflow immediately.

Three concrete use cases

1) Map the main viewpoints on a topic and open the linked sources. 2) Find primary documents or official announcements to verify a date-sensitive claim. 3) Build a reading list, then read the original sources before citing them.

Potential advantages

Research-oriented workflow; visible references can make source checking easier; useful for discovering relevant pages. For a fair test, use the same brief, source material and success criteria when comparing tools. Record the time needed to reach an acceptable result, not just the first response. A tool that produces a fast draft may still require substantial editing, fact-checking, formatting or human approval.

Limitations and trade-offs

A citation may not support every sentence; search coverage can be incomplete; source quality still requires human judgement. Do not treat generated text, images, summaries, recommendations or code as automatically correct. Check names, figures, citations, calculations, accessibility, spelling and any claim that could affect a person or business. Keep a human reviewer responsible for final decisions.

Alternatives to compare

Two alternatives worth evaluating are Google Search and ChatGPT. They may suit different budgets, workflows or preferences; this directory does not rank one as universally best. Compare the current free access, paid limits, export options, collaboration, supported languages, cancellation terms and data controls directly on each official website. Do not assume that a free tier includes commercial rights or the same features as a paid plan.

Who should not use it as their only solution

Avoid relying on Perplexity alone when a task requires guaranteed factual accuracy, regulated professional judgement, confidential information without approved safeguards, or a legally binding decision. It may also be a poor fit if you need predictable outputs without review, offline operation, a specific integration that has not been confirmed, or a licence that the provider does not clearly grant. In those situations, use a qualified professional, an approved enterprise workflow or a conventional tool with controls that meet the requirement.

Before you sign up

Read the provider's privacy and terms pages before uploading customer records, private documents, unpublished work or another person's voice or image. Check retention and training settings, access permissions, deletion options and whether your intended use is permitted. If you are evaluating a paid plan, verify the live price, billing currency, taxes, usage caps and renewal conditions at checkout; AI FACTORY does not guarantee or publish fixed prices here. Test the output with a real but non-sensitive example, then decide whether the time saved is worth the cost.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Does Perplexity show sources?

It is designed around sourced answers, but you should open and evaluate cited pages yourself.

Is every feature free?

Plan limits and feature access may change. Confirm the current offering on the official site.

Can I use it for academic research?

It can help discover sources, but verify original papers, methods, publication details, and claims you cite.
