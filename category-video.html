HOW TO CHOOSE

Choose for the task.

01

For Shorts and Reels, prioritise timeline control, captions, aspect ratios and export settings.

02

For text-to-video, test prompt adherence, clip length, consistency and credit limits.

03

For repurposing existing footage, verify you have permission to use the source video and audio.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Can AI tools make a complete YouTube Short?

Some can create drafts or assist with parts of the process, but review pacing, captions, factual accuracy and rights before publishing.

Can I repost other creators’ clips?

Only when you have the required rights or permission and comply with platform rules. Editing alone does not guarantee monetisation eligibility.
