CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Know what to expect before choosing an AI tool.

Are all listed AI tools free?

No. Some have free tiers, some are paid, and others change features by plan. We label known free tiers where appropriate; always confirm current terms on the provider’s official website.

How do I find a tool for a specific task?

Use the large search field or the bold category shortcuts near the top. Search by task words such as “remove background”, “make a video”, “research papers” or “write a resume”.

Does AI FACTORY create or own these tools?

AI FACTORY is an independent discovery directory. The listed products are owned and operated by their respective providers. We link directly to official websites.

Can I trust the pricing shown here?

Pricing labels are a quick starting point, not a promise. Providers may change free limits, availability, regions and plan details, so check the official pricing page before relying on a feature.

Does the search send my query to an AI service?

The current directory search and filters run in your browser; they do not send your search terms to an AI FACTORY backend. See the Privacy Policy for more details.
