HOW TO CHOOSE

Choose for the task.

01

For web research, check whether answers include accessible, relevant citations.

02

For academic work, inspect paper details, methods and publication context rather than relying on summaries.

03

For private documents, read data-retention and training policies before uploading sensitive files.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Are AI research answers always accurate?

No. Citations can be incomplete or misinterpreted. Open the original sources and verify key claims.

Can I upload confidential research?

Only after checking the provider’s data handling, retention, access controls and your organisation’s rules.
