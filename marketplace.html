Trust & safety

Know what has actually been checked.

A badge should explain a specific check — never promise that an asset is risk-free.

01 / CONTACT

Contact confirmed

Confirms access to an email or contact method. Not legal identity.

02 / IDENTITY

Identity checked

Only shown after a documented identity check is actually completed.

03 / OWNERSHIP

Ownership evidence

Records which control evidence was reviewed and when.

04 / LISTING

Listing reviewed

Checks claims, price, rights, included items and recurring costs.
