Buyer guide: choose the right asset

Start by choosing what you need, then compare the total cost, ownership evidence, included items and support terms before paying.

  • Check what the tool actually does and whether a demo is available.
  • Confirm free limits, recurring fees, data handling and cancellation terms.
  • Do not share passwords or send money outside an officially supported transaction flow.

Buyer protection reminder: a profile or listing check is not a guarantee of quality, income, legal ownership or future performance.

Profile verification — visible and honest

Buyer and seller profiles must show only the checks actually completed. A verified profile does not automatically mean a product is safe, profitable or legally transferable.

1. Email / contact confirmedConfirms access to a contact method. It is not proof of legal identity.
2. Identity checkedFor sellers and higher-risk actions, use a suitable provider/process; keep documents private and access-restricted.
3. Asset ownership evidenceCheck relevant domain, product, code or account control; record scope and date of review.
4. Listing reviewedReview claims, price, rights, included assets, costs and transfer requirements before publishing.
Important: These are planned verification stages, not live verification services. This preview does not collect identity documents, store profiles or issue verification badges.

A safer process, step by step

These are the planned controls. They must be implemented and tested before the marketplace accepts payments.

Seller identity

Confirm contact details and apply proportionate identity/business checks before granting verified status.

Ownership evidence

Review control of the domain, product, code or account being offered. Do not rely on screenshots alone.

Listing review

Check claims, price, included assets, recurring costs, restrictions and known risks; record what was actually checked.

Protected transaction

Only launch checkout after legal, payment-provider, server-side verification, refund and dispute flows are ready.
