Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

AI FACTORY is an independent directory that helps visitors discover third-party AI tools. This policy explains the limited data handling associated with this website.

Information and browser storage

The directory does not currently require an account and does not provide forms for submitting personal information. If you save tools, their names are stored in your browser using local storage so your saved list can persist between visits. The optional first-party recommendation card also uses session storage to remember if you dismissed it for the current browser session. These preferences are stored in your browser and are not sent to an AI FACTORY account service.

Your hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, browser details and requested pages, as part of operating and securing the website.

Search and preferences

Tool search, category filters and the Smart AI Finder operate in your browser. Search terms and finder selections are not sent to an AI FACTORY backend by the current version of this site.

Third-party websites

Tool links take you to services operated by other organizations. Those services have their own privacy policies, cookies and data practices. Review their policies before uploading files, entering personal information or purchasing a service. AI FACTORY does not control third-party websites.

Advertising and analytics

The homepage includes the Google AdSense script. Whether advertisements are displayed depends on account approval and configuration. If Google serves ads, Google may process technical data and use cookies or similar technologies according to its own settings and policies. Review Google's advertising and privacy information for details. Where consent is legally required, the appropriate consent and ad-personalization controls must be configured before those ads are served. AI FACTORY does not control Google's data practices. No separate AI FACTORY analytics service is currently configured.

Children and policy updates

This directory is a general discovery resource and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We may revise this policy as the site changes; the date above will be updated when the policy is revised.
