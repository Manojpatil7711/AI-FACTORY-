About AI FACTORY

Independent AI tools discovery directory

AI FACTORY is an independent directory designed to help people discover software and AI services for writing, design, video, research, coding, audio and productivity. The goal is to make the first stage of evaluation clearer: understand what a product may help with, see practical use cases, compare alternatives and continue to the provider's official website for current details.

Our approach

We organise tools by broad tasks and publish practical orientation guides. We aim to explain who may find a tool useful, where it has limitations and what a visitor should verify before signing up or paying. Pricing, free access, availability and product features can change, so we avoid presenting indicative directory labels as guaranteed offers. The provider's own website remains the source for current terms.

Independent and transparent

AI FACTORY is not affiliated with, sponsored by or officially endorsed by the third-party providers listed in the directory. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. A listing is not a recommendation for every person or every use case, and inclusion does not mean that a product has been independently tested or certified.

How to evaluate a tool

Start with your actual task and the sensitivity of the information you plan to share. Test with non-sensitive material, compare the output with your current workflow, check important facts and inspect privacy, licensing, billing and cancellation terms. For high-stakes decisions, use qualified human judgement and authoritative sources rather than relying on generated output alone.
