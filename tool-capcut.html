A PRACTICAL START

How to get started with CapCut.

  1. Create a project and set the canvas to the target platform's aspect ratio.
  2. Arrange only footage you have permission to use, then trim to the beat and add readable captions.
  3. Review audio levels, spelling, transitions, export resolution, and music or footage rights before posting.
CHECK BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Important limits to check.

  • Some effects, AI features, cloud tools, and export options can vary by device, region, and plan.
  • Use licensed, original, or otherwise authorised footage and music.
  • Platform copyright rules and monetisation eligibility are separate from the editing app.

We intentionally avoid exact prices or limits here because provider terms can change. Verify current pricing, free-tier limits, privacy, and licensing on the official website before purchasing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Is CapCut free?

Some features may be free and others paid or limited. Check the current app and official plan details.

Can it make captions?

Caption features are available in supported workflows, but languages, accuracy, and limits may vary. Review every caption.

Will editing another person's clip make it monetisable?

Not automatically. Permission, copyright, platform reused-content rules, and meaningful original contribution all matter.
