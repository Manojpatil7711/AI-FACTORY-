HOW TO CHOOSE

Choose for the task.

01

For narration, compare naturalness, pronunciation, language coverage and commercial voice rights.

02

For podcasts, test noise reduction, loudness consistency and how much editing remains.

03

For AI music, check whether your plan grants the rights you need for publishing or monetisation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Can I monetise AI voice or music?

That depends on the provider’s current terms, the plan used, the source material and platform policies. Verify the licence first.

Should I upload another person’s voice?

Only with appropriate consent and rights. Do not clone or imitate someone’s voice deceptively.
