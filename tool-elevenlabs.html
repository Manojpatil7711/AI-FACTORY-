A PRACTICAL START

How to get started with ElevenLabs.

  1. Prepare a clean script and choose the language, voice, and delivery style available to you.
  2. Generate a short sample and review pronunciation, pacing, emotion, and names before producing the full version.
  3. Check usage rights, consent, disclosure, and commercial terms before distributing the audio.
CHECK BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Important limits to check.

  • Voice, language, usage quotas, and commercial rights may depend on the plan and feature.
  • Never clone or imitate a real person's voice without appropriate permission and legal rights.
  • Review generated audio for pronunciation errors and misleading impersonation risks.
