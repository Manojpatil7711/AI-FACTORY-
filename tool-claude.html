PRACTICAL EVALUATION GUIDE

How to use Claude with care.

Who it may suit: Writers developing a structured draft; analysts reviewing lengthy material they are allowed to share; teams that want a conversational partner for planning and editing. This guide describes possible workflows, not a promise that every feature is available to every account. Claude is a product from Anthropic; check the provider's own documentation for current capabilities, supported regions, plan limits and terms. The right choice depends on the task, your experience, the sensitivity of the material and how much review you can provide. Begin with a small, low-risk test rather than moving an important workflow immediately.

Three concrete use cases

1) Ask it to critique the structure of a draft against a rubric you provide. 2) Summarise a permitted report, then check every key statement against the original. 3) Develop a project plan with assumptions, risks and unanswered questions clearly separated.

Potential advantages

Useful for iterative writing and structured analysis; can work from supplied context; supports a review-and-revision workflow. For a fair test, use the same brief, source material and success criteria when comparing tools. Record the time needed to reach an acceptable result, not just the first response. A tool that produces a fast draft may still require substantial editing, fact-checking, formatting or human approval.

Limitations and trade-offs

It may misread documents or omit nuance; generated reasoning is not independent verification; limits and tools vary across plans. Do not treat generated text, images, summaries, recommendations or code as automatically correct. Check names, figures, citations, calculations, accessibility, spelling and any claim that could affect a person or business. Keep a human reviewer responsible for final decisions.

Alternatives to compare

Two alternatives worth evaluating are ChatGPT and Gemini. They may suit different budgets, workflows or preferences; this directory does not rank one as universally best. Compare the current free access, paid limits, export options, collaboration, supported languages, cancellation terms and data controls directly on each official website. Do not assume that a free tier includes commercial rights or the same features as a paid plan.

Who should not use it as their only solution

Avoid relying on Claude alone when a task requires guaranteed factual accuracy, regulated professional judgement, confidential information without approved safeguards, or a legally binding decision. It may also be a poor fit if you need predictable outputs without review, offline operation, a specific integration that has not been confirmed, or a licence that the provider does not clearly grant. In those situations, use a qualified professional, an approved enterprise workflow or a conventional tool with controls that meet the requirement.

Before you sign up

Read the provider's privacy and terms pages before uploading customer records, private documents, unpublished work or another person's voice or image. Check retention and training settings, access permissions, deletion options and whether your intended use is permitted. If you are evaluating a paid plan, verify the live price, billing currency, taxes, usage caps and renewal conditions at checkout; AI FACTORY does not guarantee or publish fixed prices here. Test the output with a real but non-sensitive example, then decide whether the time saved is worth the cost.
