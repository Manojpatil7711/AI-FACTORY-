QUICK ANSWERS

Before you join

Can I pay for a plan today?

No. This is an early-access proposal page. Checkout and subscriptions are not implemented. Do not send money or share payment credentials.

Are these prices final and GST-inclusive?

No. All prices are proposed planning figures, not offers for sale. Final pricing, applicable GST, invoices, refunds and cancellation terms must be confirmed before launch.

Who can join the first launch?

The planned first market is India. We intend to validate demand with Indian buyers and sellers before considering other countries. This page does not technically verify residency.

Does Premium guarantee sales or safe deals?

No. Paid placement cannot guarantee buyers, revenue, returns or transaction safety. Any future transaction protection will be described only after it is implemented and independently reviewed.
