HOW TO CHOOSE

Choose for the task.

01

For first drafts and brainstorming, compare general assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

02

For grammar, rewriting or tone, compare dedicated writing tools and check language support.

03

For research-based writing, prefer tools that expose sources and verify important claims before publishing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Which AI writing tool should beginners try?

Start with a general assistant that has a usable free tier, then compare its output on your own task.

Can AI-written content rank on Google?

There is no automatic ranking benefit from using AI. Publish helpful, original content, verify facts and follow Google Search policies.
